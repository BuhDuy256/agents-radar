### Author renames screenshots locally with Gemma 4 and catches a test password
The author tried Lazy Screenshot Namer to rename seven screenshots instead of spending 10–15 minutes doing it by hand. Gemma 4 running locally through Ollama looked at each image, described it, suggested a short name, and flagged one containing a fake password set up for the test. ### Astra reads and rewrites weights without gradients, lifting MATH-500 to 48.1%
According to the MIT CSAIL authors, frontier LLMs can read and write neural network weights natively. GPT-6 Astra identified which of four games a PPO-trained network played from its 17,253 raw weights, with no tools. Asked to improve Qwen3.5-0.8B without gradients, Astra edited 0.016% of weights, raising MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% under the same sampling settings and tripling IFEval. The piece reports early results on small networks and one language model, not a general claim that LLM weight editing beats optimizers.

### AI SDK 7.0.163 added ping and steering-message, breaking exhaustive switches
Adding `ping` and `steering-message` to the stream-part union in a patch release made an exhaustive switch over `fullStream` fail type checking under TypeScript 7.0.2, even though nothing breaks at runtime.
