### Erase a chatbot conversation from every store, not just the inbox
The author traced one support enquiry through chat session, contact record, helpdesk ticket, emailed transcript, export file, knowledge base entry, retry queue and provider or backup copies, then wrote an ~80-line in-memory simulation of erasure. Without a tombstone and queue cancel, a retried handoff recreated a deleted ticket as T-2. Adding a tombstone keyed by an opaque HMAC and cancelling queued jobs produced zero tickets; a second tombstone check after ticket creation catches jobs already in flight. The limit: provider logs and backups can only be handled through contracts and retention terms.
