### A 125-run prompt-injection test on an agent found a gap in the test, not the defenses
The author ran 125 prompt-injection attacks (25 attacks, 5 runs each) against GAWE, an engine where a tool-less reader model extracts findings, a separate planner never sees the document, and a YAML policy engine defaults to deny. The model was not fooled in 122 decidable runs and no attacker step ran in 125, but because the model resisted, policy, approvals and plan validation were never exercised. A 10 November rerun with models scripted to be fooled let 10 of 38 attacker steps through, all allowed by the written policy. Limits: the author wrote and fixed the attacks, and CUAD may be in training data.

### An operator survey turned a keyless agent-identity idea into JWTs with fast revocation
The author proposed per-agent cryptographic identity for MCP servers, then asked operators who run them what they needed. Most of the first design failed: a free, self-made agent ID deters no abuse, several operators already revoke by user key, and three builders refused a hosted credential wallet. What came out instead is a boring JWT from an issuer the server verifies against a public JWKS, with a revoked-agent-ID list cached up to 60 seconds for reads and checked live for writes, plus separate scopes for send, delete and spend. Rollout is observe-only first, logging tokens without blocking. The author is looking for one or two operators of notes, memory or document servers to test on read tools, about 30 minutes with a snippet and two settings.
