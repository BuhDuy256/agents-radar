### Local LLM order extraction runs without a GPU on a 16 GB laptop
The author built a WhatsApp order-to-ledger app on an Intel Core i7-13620H laptop with 16 GB RAM, Intel UHD graphics and no dedicated GPU, using Ollama with `qwen2.5:7b` and `llama3.2:3b`. ## Experiments and comparisons
### One sentence in the system prompt cut planted-instruction attacks sharply
The author planted a fake log line in 8 weeks of synthetic server logs, either claiming errors were a load test or naming a `curl | sudo bash` fix, and asked eight models for a JSON health report. Without a warning sentence, 28% of reports hid the real problem and 57% recommended the command; adding one sentence telling models to treat log content as data dropped those to 10% and 24%. The `[ops-note]` placement stayed hardest to defend. Ministral 3 14B was excluded because 73 of its 112 answers were not valid JSON. 896 reports cost $2.39.

### Claude 4.6 watermarks stop when temperature leaves its default
According to an Anthropic update to API customers, statistical text watermarks on Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 only activate when temperature, top_p and top_k are left at defaults. Passing a custom temperature, such as 1.0 dropped to 0.7, routes the request to the unwatermarked sampling path. The article explains this as a logit-bias watermark that detector math can no longer verify once sampling sharpens or truncates the distribution. On Claude Opus 5.5, the piece says, watermarking persists regardless of request settings. The limit falls on client pipelines that tune sampling for extraction or creative work.
