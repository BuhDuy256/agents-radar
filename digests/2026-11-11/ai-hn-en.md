### Amazon says it replaced Sonnet 4.6 with Strands Decider on ticket labeling for 98% less cost
Two Amazon engineers wrote that they swapped Claude Sonnet 4.6 for Strands Decider E4B, a decision model that returns a probability per option instead of generating text, on an internal ticketing system with 20 label categories. On a test set the E4B build beat the production Sonnet model, and in a three-way live switch Amazon measured average call time falling from 4,215 ms to 897 ms and total tokens per ticket from 6,724 to 2,010. Amazon lists cost per million tickets at about $22,200 for Sonnet versus about $500 for the decision model. The authors say the approach only fits closed choices from a known catalog where useful context fits in a few thousand tokens.
