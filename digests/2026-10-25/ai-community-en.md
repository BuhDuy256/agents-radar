### A 1.5B model gave unstable yes/no verdicts on the same finding
The author ran a local auditor that passes each code finding to Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B-Instruct with six lines of context and one yes/no question, about twelve seconds per question on a laptop CPU. He asked Gemma 2 2B IT the same question about the same code three times in isolated contexts and got NO, NO, YES, even though the bug was present. He therefore treats the model as a hint, not a verdict: locations come from deterministic checks, and "UNSURE" is a valid answer. He states the firing threshold and flip rate under prompt shift are not yet measured.
