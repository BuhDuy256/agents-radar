### Checking cited_text catches numbers a valid URL misses
After manually opening 56 links from three scans, the author found three errors: a market figure ("32 million homes") unsupported by any source, a complaint page listed as a competitor's website, and a blog post as a competitor. Valid URLs proved pages existed, not that they contained the claimed numbers. The fix: keep the `cited_text` passage the search API already returns, then require every number in a market sentence to appear in the cited passage. Limit: 150-character passages are short, and the source may round differently.
