### Anthropic releases a shorter Lean 4 proof of GRH for Dirichlet L-functions

Anthropic published a Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions, built on Mathlib using only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The repo offers a readable edition of about 300,000 lines and a compact edition of about 76,000 lines covering the same five theorems. The proof is based on Levent Alpöge's, with Claude, and was produced with Anthropic colleagues. Building needs Lean v4.35.0 and a specific Mathlib commit, taking one to two hours on 12 cores; the proof has passed machine checks only, no mathematician has read it through, and the repo is not maintained.
