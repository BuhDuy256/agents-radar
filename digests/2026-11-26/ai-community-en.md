### A 2B decider gate stopped 7 of 11 bad refund requests; Nova Pro stopped 9
The author tested Strands Decider 2B as a tool gate on 16 refund cases against Amazon Nova Pro as a judge. The gate runs on `before_tool_call`, asks three questions, and returns Proceed, Guide, or Deny. ### Gemma 4 tool-call accuracy depended on sampling settings, not model size
The author ran E2B, E4B, and 12B Gemma 4 models, each a 4-bit QAT build on an 8 GB RTX 3050 through llama.cpp, on 840 Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard problems across four settings. At temperature 0 with thinking off, the three scored 748, 758, and 756 of 840, with E2B fastest at 0.84 seconds per problem. Google's sampling settings with thinking off cost E4B 60 problems and E2B 46. The author recommends E2B with thinking off at temperature 0, or E4B/E2B with thinking on under Google's settings.

### An agent audit design was rebuilt so constraints cannot be authored by the agent
The author's original audit-record design had the agent declare its own constraint, which meant a steered agent could omit it. After nine comments from another reader, the author proposes moving the constraint to operator config so a record citing no policy revision is itself the alert, and adding argument provenance: mark whether each consequential argument came from the standing task or only from untrusted input. Untrusted-origin values are not refused but cannot be used on first appearance, with a cooling period and a second channel. The rule still fails on takeover, where identity and source both check out.
