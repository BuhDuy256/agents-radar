### Scripts beat markdown for boxing a coding agent into repeatable actions
The author argues that telling an agent to read style guides leaves it too much judgment, and that scripts, Makefile targets and hooks turn preferences into pass/fail checks. ### Qwen thinking loops dropped from 93% to 33% after a weight edit
An MIT-affiliated team reported that GPT-6 Astra edited about 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights, subtracting a repetition-associated direction in layers 15 and 19, with gradients and optimizers forbidden.
